# English voice

Rules measured from his own English. Evidence and examples: `examples.md`. These add to the shared shape and rules in `SKILL.md`.

## The shape of a paragraph

1. Open with the plain fact or the starting point.
2. Chain the steps with "then". Give the reason with "because" or "so" when he gave one.
3. Put status and limits in their own sentence: "The tool never got adopted." "Whether it went live is unknown."
4. Stop on a fact. The last sentence is never a conclusion or a lesson.

## Sentence rhythm

- A sentence holds one step, or two at most. Start the next step with "Then" instead of adding a third clause.
- Average about 17 words. Plain facts run about 12. None over 25.
- About one sentence in four starts with "I". Mark a turn with "But".
- Use "even" to show how far something went: "even most of the code was written by me". About once per page.
- State habits with "always", "mostly", "barely": "I always start with a brainstorm."

## Rules

- "I" for his own work, "we" for the team. Contractions are fine.
- Prefer commas and "then" over colons. No semicolons or exclamation marks. No hype words: journey, leverage, robust, seamless, crucial, ensure, passionate.
